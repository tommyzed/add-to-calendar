Privacy Policy

Add to Calendar • Effective Date: August 30, 2026

1. Overview

Add to Calendar (the "Application", accessible at https://add-to-calendar.egodevnull.com) is an open-source productivity utility created and maintained by EGO /dev/null. The application allows users to extract event information (such as title, dates, times, and location) from uploaded screenshots or manual text entry and seamlessly add those events to their Google Calendar.

Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the Application.

2. Information We Collect

The Application processes only the minimum data required to perform its functions:

  • Images and Text Provided by You: When you upload a screenshot or enter event details manually, that data is processed solely to extract event fields (summary, start time, end time, location).
  • Google Account Authorization Data: When you connect your Google Account, the Application receives an OAuth 2.0 authorization token and refresh token used exclusively to interact with the Google Calendar API on your behalf. We do not collect or store your Google account password.

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Event Parsing (AI Extraction): Uploaded image data is sent securely to the Google Gemini API to analyze the screenshot and return structured event details.
  • Google Calendar Integration: Upon your explicit confirmation by clicking "Add to Calendar", the Application uses the Google Calendar API to insert the event into your primary calendar and provide a link to view the created event.

4. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Add to Calendar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We only request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events scope, which is the minimum scope required to create and insert calendar events requested by you.
  • We do not read, view, modify, or delete your existing unrelated calendar events.
  • Google user data is never used for serving advertisements, training generalized AI/ML models, or sold to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Retention

  • No Server-Side Storage: We do not operate databases that store your uploaded images or calendar contents. Image data is processed ephemerally in memory.
  • Local Storage: Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device in your browser's localStorage to keep you signed in between sessions. You can delete these at any time by clicking "Sign Out" within the app or clearing your browser storage.

6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only transmitted to:

  • Google Gemini API: To extract event details from uploaded images.
  • Google Calendar API: To create the event in your personal Google Calendar account upon your request.

7. User Control and Revoking Access

You have complete control over your data and access permissions:

  • You can disconnect your Google Calendar at any time by clicking the Sign Out button in the Application.
  • You can permanently revoke the Application's access at any time through your Google Account Security Settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
